# Series: <name>

Copy this folder to `series/<name>/` for each account or recurring format.

- **Account:** @handle (or "not created yet")
- **Audience:** who watches, what they already know
- **Promise:** what every video in this series gives them
- **Voice:** tone, person (I / we / you), words to avoid
- **Language:** spoken and on-screen language
- **Format:** length range, structure (hook → beats → payoff → CTA), recurring visual elements
- **Narration:** voice used with `hyperframes tts`, or "none, text only"
- **Cadence:** how often it posts
- **Do not:** topics, claims or styles that are off limits
